# 🎨 India Post Mascot: Design & Branding Guidelines (Indian Storybook Edition)

This document outlines the core deliverables, communication goals, skill requirements, and platform categorization guidelines for the India Post Mascot Design Contest. The primary aesthetic directive for all outputs is to create a clear, simple mascot image heavily inspired by classic Indian story pictures, folk art, and traditional storytelling aesthetics.

---

## 📌 Deliverables

### 1. Primary Design Requirement (Core Deliverable)
The heart of the project is developing the official mascot and visual personality for India Post. 
*   **Mascot Design** (Main Deliverable - focused on clean, simple, storybook lines)[cite: 2]
*   **Brand Character Design**[cite: 2]
*   **Brand Representation**[cite: 2]
*   **Visual Brand Ambassador**[cite: 2]
*   **Character Illustration** (Must resemble Indian storybook illustrations, e.g., Panchatantra or Amar Chitra Katha styles)[cite: 2]
*   **Digital Character Artwork**[cite: 2]

### 2. Supporting Deliverables
*   **Mascot Name**[cite: 2]
*   **Transliteration** (English / Hindi translation of the name)[cite: 2]
*   **Concept Note** (The creative rationale and logic behind the design, specifically detailing the traditional Indian visual influences)[cite: 2]
*   **High-Resolution Digital Artwork**[cite: 2]

---

## 🗣️ Core Communication Goals
The mascot must visually communicate the following themes[cite: 2] using simplified, flat, and vibrant Indian design languages:
*   🤝 **Trust & Reliability**[cite: 2]
*   📮 **Public Service**[cite: 2]
*   👥 **Inclusivity**[cite: 2]
*   🇮🇳 **Indian Culture & Heritage** (Achieved through story-picture aesthetics, bold motifs, and relatable cultural attire)[cite: 2]
*   ⚡ **Digital Innovation**[cite: 2]
*   😊 **Friendly Personality**[cite: 2]
*   ✨ **Memorability** (Ensured through simple, easy-to-draw, clean vectors)[cite: 2]
*   🌐 **Nationwide Connectivity**[cite: 2]
