# 🎨 India Post Mascot: Design & Branding Guidelines

This document outlines the core deliverables, communication goals, skill requirements, and platform categorization guidelines for the India Post Mascot Design Contest.

---

## 📌 Deliverables

### 1. Primary Design Requirement (Core Deliverable)
The heart of the project is developing the official mascot and visual personality for India Post.
*   **Mascot Design** (Main Deliverable)
*   **Brand Character Design**
*   **Brand Representation**
*   **Visual Brand Ambassador**
*   **Character Illustration**
*   **Digital Character Artwork**

### 2. Supporting Deliverables
*   **Mascot Name**
*   **Transliteration** (English / Hindi translation of the name)
*   **Concept Note** (The creative rationale and logic behind the design)
*   **High-Resolution Digital Artwork**

---

## 🗣️ Core Communication Goals
The mascot must visually communicate the following themes:
*   🤝 **Trust & Reliability**
*   📮 **Public Service**
*   👥 **Inclusivity**
*   🇮🇳 **Indian Culture & Heritage**
*   ⚡ **Digital Innovation**
*   😊 **Friendly Personality**
*   ✨ **Memorability**
*   🌐 **Nationwide Connectivity**

## 💡 Conclusion
This contest is fundamentally about creating a mascot that serves as the official visual identity and personality of India Post. The final output must be supported by cohesive branding, storytelling, and professional illustration—not just a standalone graphic.
